Written steps

A short Advent activity for children, every day from 1 to 24 December. You need this website and a notebook or the printed tracker sheet.

For parents

  1. Each day, open the website and choose Juniors (Year 5–7) or Seniors (Year 8 and above).
  2. Tap today’s date and do the activity together.
  3. Write down the secret word or words for that day.
  4. On 24 December, do the final task: make sentences using the words from the days it gives, for example Days 3, 4, 5, 7 and 9.
  5. Write the secret words in a notebook, or on the tracker sheet: download it here.
  6. After Christmas, give the notebook or tracker sheet to the teacher.

Please stay with your child while they use the website.

For teachers

  1. Send the website link to parents before 1 December.
  2. Collect the notebooks or tracker sheets after Christmas.
  3. Check that each day has its secret word or words, and look at the sentences from the 24 December task.
